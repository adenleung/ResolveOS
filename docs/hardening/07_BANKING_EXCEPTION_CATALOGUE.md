# Banking exception catalogue

Seven detailed specifications with all 20 requested dimensions are in datasets/banking-exception-catalogue.json. These are simplified synthetic operating assumptions, not bank-approved SOPs or ISO 20022 interoperability. They use stable payment/event/trace/ledger references, currency and amounts, event time versus ingestion time, settlement versus confirmation and action-specific review. No real account/customer records exist.

| Exception | Observed source / trigger | Investigation | Permitted handling | Verification requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Missing payment confirmation | Payments, ledger, confirmation, API: Settled or pending payment exceeds confirmation window | Check settlement, one posted debit, absent confirmation, ambiguity and current action policy | Replay only when settled and authorized; otherwise investigate/review | Independent confirmation processed and all protected monetary fields unchanged |
| Reconciliation mismatch | Payments and ledger: Amount or currency disagreement | Compare ledger transaction reference, amount/currency and event freshness | Escalate; do not alter balances | Reconcile authoritative source agreement; no automated correction exists |
| Duplicate business event | Confirmation source and payment/ledger: Source marks duplicate or replayed logical confirmation | Distinguish transport retry, source business duplicate and actual second debit | Review; no blind payment or confirmation replay | Check unique effect identity and absence of duplicate monetary effect |
| API processing failure | API gateway, related payment observations: 5xx or latency threshold exceeded | Determine ambiguous success, evidence gap and affected scope | Investigate/escalate; infrastructure restart not allowlisted | Independent source observations; HTTP 200 alone is insufficient |
| Missing supporting document | Workflow; proposed approved document observation: DOCUMENT_REQUIRED workflow; no approved document-source execution exists | Establish genuine document provenance and reviewer competence | Human obtains/reviews evidence; no fabricated document | Approved source confirmation would be needed; currently deferred |
| Overdue workflow | Workflow events and timestamps: Incomplete workflow exceeds deadline | Current step, deadline, earlier approvals and missing evidence | Human triage; existing durable retry only for eligible supported tasks | Observed workflow progression would be required; no invented completion |
| Contradictory evidence | Payment, confirmation, ledger and API: Incompatible current source states | Identify superseded records and uncertainty, do not infer cause from similarity | Reinvestigate or escalate; controls block inconsistent replay | Fresh independent source agreement required before any later action |

Manual operating baseline: open exception; identify payment/workflow references; retrieve ledger/confirmation/API observations; compare authoritative states and timestamps; establish missing or contradictory evidence; consult current policy and roles; prepare permitted action or escalation; obtain required action approval; perform the supported synthetic step; independently reread sources; record outcome and unresolved uncertainty. Human active effort and queue waiting must be timed separately in a future participant study. The benchmark's explicit time assumptions are modelling, not observed banking productivity.

Supported and proposed capabilities remain separate: document/workflow handlers and monetary correction are absent. Confirmation replay does not resend payment funds. Transport duplicates do not by themselves prove duplicate business effects. Similarity, recent incidents and historical summaries are not proof of causation.
